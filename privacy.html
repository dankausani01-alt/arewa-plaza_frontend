Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026  |  Effective immediately upon use of AREWA SQUARE

Your privacy matters to us. AREWA SQUARE is committed to protecting the personal information of every buyer and seller who uses our platform. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

2. Information We Collect

When you use AREWA SQUARE, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, and account type (buyer or seller).
  • Business Information (Sellers): Shop name, product categories, business location (LGA/ward), and product listings including descriptions and prices.
  • Transaction Information: Orders placed, items selected, and communications sent through the platform.
  • Device Information: Browser type, device type, and approximate location (city/region level, used to show nearby shops).
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, shops viewed, and search queries — used only to improve the platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for legitimate purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To verify seller identities and business legitimacy
  • To display shops relevant to your location
  • To facilitate communication between buyers and sellers
  • To send you important account and order notifications
  • To resolve disputes and enforce our Seller Rules
  • To improve and maintain the AREWA SQUARE platform

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

4. Information Sharing

Your information is shared only in these limited cases:

  • Between buyers and sellers: When a buyer places an order, their order details are shared with the relevant seller. Buyers choose which shops to contact.
  • With our team: KAUSANITECH staff may access account information only for verification, support, or dispute resolution purposes.
  • Legal requirements: We will share information if required by Nigerian law or to prevent fraud and illegal activity.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no internet platform can guarantee 100% security. We encourage you to use a strong password and never share your login credentials.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at arewasquare@gmail.com

7. Cookies & Local Storage

AREWA SQUARE uses browser local storage to remember your login session and cart contents. No third-party tracking cookies are used. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

AREWA SQUARE is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us immediately.
